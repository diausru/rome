Description:
Maya moved from Winnipeg to Calgary for a new job and spent $6,000. She can deduct all of it, just not all this year.

The rules (CRA line 21900, 2025):
• Your new home must be at least 40 km closer (shortest public route) to the new work location.
• Eligible: movers, the trip, temporary lodging (and more on the CRA list): $6,000 here.
• The deduction only comes from employment or self-employment income earned at the new location.

She started in November and earned $4,000 there → $4,000 deducted for 2025, the other $2,000 carried forward to 2026 pay from the same job. No carry-back.

Keep every receipt; if your employer reimbursed you, include it in income or leave that part out.

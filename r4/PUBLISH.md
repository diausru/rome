Description:
Same move, same truck: 34 km closer to work = $0 deduction; 43 km closer = the whole move counts.

The rule (CRA line 21900): your new home must be at least 40 km closer to your new work location, measured by the shortest public route.
• Old home: 52 km from the new job
• Apartment A, 18 km away → 34 km closer ✗
• Apartment B, 9 km away → 43 km closer ✓ → $4,200 of eligible costs (truck, travel, up to 15 days of temporary living)

The twist: you can only deduct it from employment or self-employment income earned at the new location. Riley started in December 2025 and earned $2,500 there → deducts $2,500 for 2025; the other $1,700 carries forward to future income at the new job.

Measure the route before you sign the lease, and keep every receipt (Form T1-M).

## Facts (REAL TAX INFORMATION MODE)

Primary source: Income Tax Act (laws-lois.justice.gc.ca, current to 2026-09-21), saved in `a5/sources/`.

| Claim | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| CRA may reassess an individual within 3 years after the original notice | ITA 152(3.1)(b), 152(4) | rule (general; exceptions exist) |
| Interest at the prescribed rate on unpaid tax after the balance-due day; individual's balance-due day = April 30 of the following year; compounded daily | ITA 161(1), 248(1), 248(11) | rule |
| Instalments required if net tax owing > $3,000 ($1,800 Quebec) for the year and either of the two preceding years; instalment interest | ITA 156.1(1), 161(2) | rule |
| No legal collection actions until 90 days after the notice is sent; after an objection, until 90 days after CRA confirms/varies | ITA 225.1(1), (1.1)(c), (2) | rule |
| Objection deadline for individuals: later of 1 year after the filing-due date and 90 days after the notice | ITA 165(1)(a) | rule |
| Example rates 20.5% federal + 12.75% Manitoba = 33.25% at ~$80K taxable income (2026) | ITA 117(2); Manitoba Budget 2025 / CRA T4127 2026 (verified for H4) | example |

Example (not a CRA rule): $15,000 of unreported T5 interest × 33.25% = $4,987.50 (shown $4,988) of a $5,000 balance; instalment bars ($2,400 / $4,200 / $5,000) are illustrative. The prescribed interest rate itself is not stated (it changes quarterly and was not verified from canada.ca, which refused connections). The example notice on screen is generic ("A NOTICE · EXAMPLE", no CRA name, crest or layout). Professional-advice territory: payment arrangements, taxpayer relief (interest/penalty cancellation), and objections with complex facts.

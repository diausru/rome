# A5 — Retention Blueprint (written before any visuals)

**Opening question:** a notice says you owe $5,000. Why, and what do you do before paying?
**Promised result:** three checks that explain most surprise balances, and the deadlines that give you time.

**First 30 s (VO → visual):**
- 0–5 s "A CRA notice says you owe five thousand dollars. Before you pay, or panic, let's investigate." → frame 0: an example notice card (generic, no CRA marks) with BALANCE $5,000 under a magnifying lens; title "YOU OWE $5,000?".
- 5–7 s "Three usual suspects." → three suspect cards slide into a line-up.
- 7–17 s Suspect 1: forgotten T5, $15,000 interest × ~33% (20.5% federal + 12.75% Manitoba) ≈ $4,988 → the lens moves to an "income added" line; a bar fills the $5,000 almost completely.
- 17–26 s Suspect 2: payment applied to another year → a payment chip slides into the wrong year column, then snaps back.
- 26–36 s Suspect 3: instalments, > $3,000 this year and one of the last two.

**Open questions carried:** after each suspect, "is that you?"; the reveal answers "how long do I have?".
**Reveal (36–46 s):** a timeline scrub April 30 → notice → +90 days (no legal collection) → objection deadline; a daily-interest ticker runs the whole time.
**Answer + next step:** open the notice, read the explanation of changes, match every slip, pay or object. Bridge to A8 (filing deadlines).
**Verified calculations and sources:** `a5/data.json` (ITA 152(3.1), 156.1, 161, 165(1), 225.1, 248(1),(11); 2026 federal and Manitoba rates). No answer is delayed: each suspect lands its point within ~6 s.

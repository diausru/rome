**Worked example (not a CRA rule):** the B4 freelancer, total tax + CPP $16,105.48 ("$16,106"); with the prior-year option and the same prior year, each payment ≈ $16,105.48 ÷ 4 = $4,026.37 → "$4,026". Threshold check: income tax alone ($5,155 + $4,227 = $9,382) is above $3,000, so the CPP threshold nuance (MoneySense) does not change the example.
**Not stated (not verified):** the instalment penalty (when interest exceeds $1,000) and the exact daily compounding wording; Québec details are left to the description.

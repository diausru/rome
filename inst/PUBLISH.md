Owe the CRA more than $3,000? You may have to pay tax in four instalments.

The rule: instalments apply if your net tax owing is over $3,000 this year AND in one of the last two years ($1,800 in Québec). Example: our freelancer's tax and CPP is about $16,106 → about $4,026 per payment (prior-year option). Due dates: the 15th of March, June, September and December.

The year-two trap: instalments can start in March while last year's balance is still due by April 30. The simple route: pay the amounts on the CRA's reminder, on time, and there's no instalment interest even if they come up short. Late or short on your own numbers? Interest runs at the prescribed rate (7% for Oct–Dec 2026).

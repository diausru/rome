Description:
The GST credit is gone. Same deposit, new name: the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB), and it's bigger.

• Replaced the GST/HST credit in July 2026: 25% more, for five years.
• Maximum for July 2026–June 2027: $679 single, $890 for a couple, plus $234 per child under 19.
• Example: a couple with two kids and family net income up to $46,432 → $890 + 2 × $234 = $1,358 a year.
• Paid in four parts: July, October, January and April (here $339.50 each).

The twist: above $46,432 of family net income, 5% of the extra comes off. $10,000 over? $500 less ($858 in the example).

It's worked out from last year's return, so file on time, every year.

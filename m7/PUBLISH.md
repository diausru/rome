Description:
Same job, same kids, and in July the Canada Child Benefit deposit changed. It happens every year, for two reasons.

1) The benefit year runs July to June, and every July the CRA recalculates it from last year's tax return. Payments from July 2026 to June 2027 use your 2025 adjusted family net income. Example: a family whose income rose from $60,000 to $70,000 in 2025 sees that raise reflected now, in July 2026.
2) The amounts are indexed to inflation every July: maximum for a child under 6 went from $7,997 to $8,157 a year ($6,748 → $6,883 for ages 6–17).

The twist: the CCB always runs a year behind your life. Lose income this year, and it only shows up next July.

When July looks different, check last year's return, not this year's paycheque.

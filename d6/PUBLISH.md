Description:
RRSP or TFSA? It isn't about which one is "better". It's your tax rate now versus later.

RRSP: contributions are deductible, so you save tax today, but withdrawals are taxed as income.
TFSA: no deduction going in, but growth and withdrawals are tax-free, and the amount you withdraw comes back as contribution room on January 1 of the next year.

Illustration: $1,000 of income that doubles, at the same 30% rate going in and coming out, leaves $1,400 in either account. A lower rate later (20%) favours the RRSP ($1,600); a higher rate later (40%) favours the TFSA ($1,400 vs $1,200).

2026 limits: TFSA $7,000 (plus unused room); RRSP 18% of last year's earned income, up to $33,810. Check your own room in CRA My Account.

Sources: CRA (canada.ca). The example is an illustration. Mechanics only, not investment advice.

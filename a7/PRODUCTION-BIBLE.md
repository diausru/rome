### Example (ILLUSTRATION)
Business records for the 2026 tax year: six years from the end of 2026 → keep until at least the end of 2032 (R1). Labelled "Example · business receipts".

### Dependencies
For individuals' personal returns, CRA's consumer pages say "at least six years after you file" (R2); the business rule counts from the end of the tax year (R1). The video states "generally six years" and uses the business rule for the dated example only.

## A. Final script
Throwing out old receipts? CRA can ask for them years later.
So how long is long enough?
Generally, six years. For business records, six years from the end of the tax year they relate to.
Business receipts for twenty twenty-six? Keep them until at least the end of twenty thirty-two.
Filed late? The six years start when you file. Disputing an assessment? Keep everything until it's resolved.
Scans and digital files count, as long as they're complete and readable.
Want to destroy them sooner? You need CRA's permission first.
Six years, digital is fine, and never toss the proof too early.
Follow for the real math.

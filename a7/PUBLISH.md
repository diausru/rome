Description:
Throwing out old receipts? CRA can ask for them years later.

Generally, keep your records for six years; for business records, six years from the end of the tax year they relate to. Example: business receipts for 2026 → keep until at least the end of 2032.
Exceptions: filed late? The six years start when you file. Disputing an assessment (objection or appeal)? Keep everything until it's resolved.
Scans and digital files count, as long as they're complete and readable. Want to destroy records sooner? You need CRA's permission first (Form T137).

Sources: CRA (canada.ca): Where to keep your records and for how long; IC78-10R. General information, not advice.

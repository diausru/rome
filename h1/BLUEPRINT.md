**Reveal (44–51 s):** a switch: NON-REFUNDABLE (only cuts tax owed → $0) vs REFUNDABLE (paid out, even at $0 tax).
**Answer + next step (52–59 s):** checklist: attach MB479 · caregiver: register first · missed one: up to 3 years back (most credits). Bridge to C1.

Description:
You owe zero tax in Manitoba. The province can still pay you. Three people, one form: MB479.

Case 1: Lena cares for her dad at home, unpaid, for more than 90 days. Once she registers with Manitoba Finance, she can claim the Primary Caregiver Tax Credit: $1,400 a year (flat, not income-tested; care level 2 or higher at home).
Case 2: a couple pays $20,000 for fertility treatment at a Manitoba clinic. The Fertility Treatment Tax Credit is 40%: $8,000 back (up to $16,000 on $40,000 of expenses, net of insurance; one spouse claims).
Case 3: a homeowner never got the Homeowners Affordability Tax Credit on the property tax bill. Claim it on the return: the lesser of $1,600 and your school taxes for 2026 ($1,500 for 2025).

The twist: these credits are refundable. Even with no tax to pay, the money still comes to you.

What to do: attach Form MB479 to your return. Caregiver? Register first. Missed one? Most MB479 credits can be claimed up to 3 years back.

Also on MB479: the personal tax credit and, for homeowners 65+, the seniors' school tax rebate (up to $235).

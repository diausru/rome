Description:
Put $2,500 a year into your child's RESP and the government adds $500. That's the Canada Education Savings Grant (CESG): 20% of the first $2,500 contributed each year, up to $7,200 per child, available until the end of the year the child turns 17.

Example: $2,500 a year from birth reaches the full $7,200 grant in year 15.
Missed years? Unused grant room carries forward: you can get up to $1,000 of grant in one year (a $5,000 contribution).
Lower-income family? The Canada Learning Bond adds up to $2,000 for children born in 2004 or later, with no contribution needed.

The catch: if your child doesn't go on to post-secondary education, the grant and the bond are repaid to the government.

Sources: ESDC and CRA (canada.ca): How much money can be added to RESPs; Canada Education Savings Grant; Canada Learning Bond. The $2,500-a-year plan is an example. General information, not financial advice.

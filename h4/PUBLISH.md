Description:
Prices went up. Your pay went up. Manitoba's tax brackets haven't moved since 2024.

Manitoba 2026 rates on taxable income: 10.8% up to $47,000 · 12.75% up to $100,000 · 17.4% above $100,000.
Since the 2025 tax year, Manitoba has frozen the indexation of those thresholds and of the basic personal amount ($15,780). No inflation increase.

Example: a raise takes taxable income from $98,000 to $102,000. $2,000 is taxed at 12.75% and $2,000 at 17.4%: $603 of Manitoba tax on that raise (federal tax not included).
With frozen lines, a raise that only matches prices can push more of your pay into a higher rate.

High incomes: from 2025 the Manitoba basic personal amount is phased out between $200,000 and $400,000 of net income.
The other side, for 2026: the Renters Affordability Tax Credit goes up to $625, and the Homeowners Affordability Tax Credit up to $1,600.

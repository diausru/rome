Description:
Retired with income over $95,323? Part of your Old Age Security goes back. Here's exactly how much, and three legal levers.

The OAS recovery tax: for 2026 income, you repay 15% of your net world income above $95,323. Example: net income of $110,000 is $14,677 over the line, so $2,201.55 is recovered, withheld from your OAS payments from July 2027 to June 2028.

Three legal levers:
1) Pension income splitting: at 65+, up to 50% of eligible pension income (including RRIF and annuity payments) can be allocated to your spouse (joint election, Form T1032).
2) TFSA withdrawals aren't income, so they don't affect OAS.
3) Delaying OAS: +0.6% for each month after 65, up to +36% at 70 (you can't get GIS while you delay).

Which lever fits depends on your whole picture. Talk to a tax professional.

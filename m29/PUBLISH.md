Three programs, one home:
• FHSA: $8,000 of room a year, $40,000 for life. Opened in 2023 and filled every year → $24,000. Deducted going in, tax-free coming out.
• Home Buyers' Plan: now up to $60,000 from your RRSPs (was $35,000). Example withdrawal: $35,000. You can use the FHSA and the HBP for the same home.
• Home buyers' amount (line 31270): $10,000 × 14.5% = $1,450 off federal tax (non-refundable).

The twist: the FHSA is never paid back. The HBP is a loan to yourself: 1/15 a year (here ≈ $2,333), starting in the 5th year after the withdrawal year (2030 for a 2025 withdrawal, under the relief for first withdrawals in 2022–2025).

Worried a raise will push you into a higher tax bracket? In Canada, a higher bracket never taxes your whole income. Each rate only taxes its own slice.

One real exception: income-tested benefits like the Canada Child Benefit shrink as family income rises (reduction starts above $38,237 of adjusted family net income for July 2026 – June 2027). On benefits? Run your numbers with a professional.

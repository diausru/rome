Description:
Low pay, little tax, maybe none at all. The CRA can still pay you up to $1,633.

The Canada Workers Benefit (CWB) is a refundable credit for low-income workers: it comes back to you even if you owe nothing.
• 2025: up to $1,633 single, $2,813 for a family; working income must be over $3,000.
• It fades: single, above $26,855 of adjusted net income, 15% of the extra comes off (nothing left at $37,742).
• Example: Leo earned $30,000 → $3,145 over × 15% = $471.75 less → about $1,161.

The twist: you don't have to wait for the refund. Up to half can be paid to you in advance during the year (Advanced CWB).

Claim it when you file: Schedule 6, line 45300.

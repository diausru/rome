Description:
Two first-time buyers can put up to $200,000 toward one home, with no tax on the way out. Here's how it stacks.

• FHSA: up to $8,000 a year, $40,000 lifetime. Contributions are deductible; qualifying withdrawals for a first home are tax-free.
• Home Buyers' Plan (HBP): up to $60,000 from your RRSP, repaid over 15 years.
• Per person up to $100,000; two qualifying buyers, up to $200,000.

The twist: you can use the FHSA and the HBP for the same home. For a first HBP withdrawal from 2026 to 2028, repayments start in the fifth year after (CRA example: withdraw in 2026, first repayment year 2031).

Plus the home buyers' amount (line 31270): up to $10,000 claimed as a credit. Check every condition before you withdraw.

Description:
Can you write it off? Before anything else, CRA asks: did you spend it to earn income?

The general rule (CRA Guide T4002): a reasonable current expense you incur to earn business income is deductible; personal costs are not. Mixed use? Claim only the business part (example: a $1,200 phone bill used 60% for the business = $720).
Something that lasts for years, like a laptop or a car, isn't deducted all at once; it's claimed over time through capital cost allowance (CCA).
Business meals and entertainment: generally only 50%.
On a salary? You can deduct employment expenses only if your contract requires you to pay them (your employer signs Form T2200).
And keep the proof: records generally for six years.

Sources: CRA (canada.ca): T4002, line 8523, T4044, Business records. The phone example is an illustration. General information, not advice.

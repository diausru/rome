## A. Final script
Renting out a property? One deduction can bite you when you sell.
First, what you can deduct.
Mortgage interest, property tax, insurance, repairs, utilities. Not the principal, and not your own labour.
Fix a wooden step? Deduct it now. Upgrade it to concrete? That's a capital cost.
Those are claimed slowly, as capital cost allowance: four percent a year on most rental buildings, half in year one. Never on land.
It can't create or increase a rental loss. And it's optional.
The trap: claim it in full for ten years on a three-hundred-thousand-dollar building. That's about ninety-six thousand.
Sell for more than you paid, and it all comes back as recapture: fully taxed income, not half like a capital gain.
CCA is often a deferral, not a gift. Plan before you claim.
Next: money moves to make before December thirty-first. Follow so you don't miss it.

## Description
Priya earns $75,000 in Toronto and pays $12,705 of income tax. How much goes to Ottawa, and how much to Ontario? And if she moved to Calgary or Vancouver, which part wouldn't change by a single dollar?

• One return, two taxes: the CRA collects provincial tax for every province except Quebec (Form 428)
• The split at $75,000: federal $8,259 · Ontario $4,446 (incl. $750 Ontario Health Premium) — about two thirds federal
• Move provinces: federal stays $8,259; provincial ON $4,446 · AB $4,010 · BC $3,661
• Quebec: second return + 16.5% Quebec abatement
• The split isn't fixed: $40K, $150K, $250K and the Ontario surtax
• A deduction cuts both taxes ($1,000 RRSP ≈ $205 federal + $91 Ontario); a credit cuts only its own side

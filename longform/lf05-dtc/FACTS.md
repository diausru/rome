# LF-05 facts — Disability Tax Credit: how to apply (checked 2026-10-12; pages in sources/)
| Claim | Source (canada.ca) | Year |
|---|---|---|
| DTC is non-refundable; unused part not refunded, may be transferred | about-dtc, claiming-dtc | current |
| 2 steps: apply (you + medical practitioner), then claim on the return | about-dtc | current |
| Eligibility: severe & prolonged impairment in 1 category (marked restriction), significant limitations in 2+ (cumulative effect), or life-sustaining therapy; certified by a medical practitioner | eligible-dtc | current |
| Who certifies: medical doctor & nurse practitioner (all); optometrist (vision); audiologist (hearing); occupational therapist (walking, feeding, dressing); physiotherapist (walking); psychologist (mental functions); speech-language pathologist (speaking) | how-apply-dtc | current |
| Digital: Part A in CRA account/phone → reference number (valid 12 months, used once) → practitioner submits Part B online; both parts same method; applicant cannot fill Part B | how-apply-dtc | current |
| Apply any time; apply before filing to avoid delaying assessment | how-apply-dtc | current |
| CRA may write to practitioner for more info; 60 business days to respond | cra-review-dtc | current |
| Notice of determination: permanent, temporary or denied; options if denied: review/new docs, objection, call | cra-decision-dtc | current |
| Disability amount 2025: $10,138 (18+); under 18: + $5,914 supplement = $16,052 | claiming-dtc | 2025 |
| Past years: up to 10 years; tick box on the form or ask in writing / change returns | claiming-dtc, cra-review-dtc | current |
| Supporting family member list + basic necessities test; lines 31600 / 31800 / 32600 | claiming-dtc | current |
| Practitioner fee may be a medical expense (lines 33099/33199) | claiming-dtc | current |
| From July 14, 2026: no DTC applications via "submit documents"; from Sept 8, 2026: pre-2023 T2201 versions not accepted | CRA tax tip, June 16 2026 | 2026 |
| DTC eligibility may help access RDSP, CWB disability supplement, child disability benefit, Canada disability benefit; other programs' eligibility does not affect DTC | about-dtc | current |
| Federal value 2025: lowest rate 14.5% for 2025 (14% from 2026) → $10,138 × 14.5% = $1,470 (calc) | CRA "What's new for 2025"; Finance report on lowest rate | 2025 |
Dependencies: provincial disability amounts differ by province (not shown as a fixed number); whether a person qualifies depends on the practitioner's certification and CRA's review — never implied.
| Marked restriction (e.g. walking, mental functions): unable, or takes 3× longer than someone of similar age without the impairment, even with therapy/medication/devices; all or almost all of the time (generally 90%+); lasted / expected to last ≥ 12 continuous months | eligible-dtc/walking, mental-functions | current |
| Life-sustaining therapy: needed ≥ 2 times per week AND an average ≥ 14 hours per week; ≥ 12 months | eligible-dtc/life-sustaining-therapy | current |
| Cumulative effect: significant limitations in 2+ categories together equivalent to one marked restriction; 90%+ of the time; ≥ 12 months; excludes life-sustaining therapy | eligible-dtc/cumulative-effect | current |

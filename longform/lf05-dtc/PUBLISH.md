## Description
A form your doctor fills out could be worth thousands of dollars — sometimes going back up to ten years. We follow an example family (Maria and her son Liam, 15) through the Disability Tax Credit: what it is, the three ways to qualify, which medical practitioners can certify which impairment, the 2-part application, what changed in 2026, the CRA review, the decision, past years, and what to do if the answer is no.

Key figures (CRA, 2025 tax year): disability amount $10,138 (18+); under 18: + $5,914 supplement = $16,052. Federal value at 14.5% ≈ $1,470 for the adult amount (provinces add their own credit).

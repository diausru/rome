## Description
Two people, the same side hustle, the same $10,000 of profit. Sam has a $60,000 day job and keeps $6,145. Priya has a $90,000 day job and keeps $7,009, which is $864 more.

The reason is self-employed CPP: 11.9% (both halves) up to the 2026 ceilings of $74,600 and $85,000. Priya's job already maxed it out.

The video also covers:
• the same $10,000 at four day-job salaries ($40K / $60K / $90K / $130K)
• raise vs side hustle (a raise keeps about $300 more for Sam)
• what $3,000 of business expenses really saves (about $1,156)
• the April bill: about $3,855 due April 30 (filing deadline June 15), setting aside about 39¢ per dollar, and the $3,000 instalment test
• the $30,000 GST/HST small-supplier line, which counts sales, not profit

The numbers use 2026 federal and Ontario rates for a single filer with no other income. Sam and Priya are examples, not real people. General information, not advice. Sources: canada.ca.

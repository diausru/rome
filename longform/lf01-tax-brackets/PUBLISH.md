## Description
Canadian tax brackets explained with real numbers. You make $90,000 in Ontario and you're "in the 30% bracket." So does 30% of your pay disappear? No — and the real number is closer to 19%.

In this video we follow three Canadians — $45,000, $90,000 and $150,000 — through the 2026 federal and Ontario tax brackets, step by step, with real numbers: bracket rate vs real rate, what credits do, what each person keeps after income tax, CPP and EI, and what changes if the same salary is earned in Alberta or Manitoba.

Examples are fictional, single employees with employment income only, no RRSP or other deductions. Your result depends on your credits, deductions, other income and province. General information, not tax advice.

Sources: CRA 2026 federal rates and indexation (T4127, 122nd/123rd ed.); provincial 2026 rates (Ontario, Alberta, Manitoba) as published in T4127. Calculations: TAX SECRETS CANADA, 2026.

Rental Market Survey Data Tables

Excel - Publication

Find the results from CMHC’s 2025 Rental Market Survey (presented in Microsoft Excel tables). These data tables complement the analysis found in the Rental Market Report.

Data contained in these tables include:

vacancy rate estimates
average rents
turnover rates
universe counts

These results are available for Canada, all provinces, and major centres.

For 17 Canadian centres, the tables also include results from our Condominium Apartment Survey. This survey covers condominium apartments offered for rent on the secondary rental market in these centres.

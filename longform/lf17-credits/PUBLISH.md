## Description
Three friends, the same $1,000 tax break: Nina saves $8, Omar $296, Priya $434. Change one word — deduction to credit — and the results change completely.

• Where each works: deductions come off income, credits come off the tax
• A $1,000 deduction = your marginal rate ($8 / $296 / $434 at $14K / $65K / $120K, Ontario 2026)

• The catch: non-refundable credits stop at $0; refundable credits pay even with no tax
• Bonus: deductions lower net income (CCB, OAS clawback); credits don't

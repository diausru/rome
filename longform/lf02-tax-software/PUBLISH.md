Seven CRA-certified tax programs. Do-it-yourself prices from $0 to $80. So which one should YOU use?

We run a face-off with four Canadians — a student, an employee with one T4, an investor and a self-employed landlord — and show what each would pay in Wealthsimple Tax, TurboTax, H&R Block, UFile, StudioTax, CloudTax and GenuTax, using each company's published prices for the 2025 tax year.

The twist: certified software applies the same tax law. What you pay for is forms, help and audit protection — not a different tax result.

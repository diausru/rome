SimpleFile

A free service offered by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) to eligible individuals with a lower income and a simple tax situation to file personal income tax and benefit return.

0Free Secure Fast
If you still need to file a 2025 tax return

In September 2026, the CRA will send eligible individuals an invitation to do their 2025 taxes using SimpleFile. If you do not receive an invitation, you may still be able to use SimpleFile Digital.

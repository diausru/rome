NETFILE

Use NETFILE to send your return directly to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) over the Internet.

Income tax preparation software companies must seek NETFILE certification from the Canada Revenue Agency (the "CRA") for tax preparation software products to be used in conjunction with CRA's NETFILE electronic tax filing service. For the 2025 taxation year, NETFILE certification of GenuTax Standard from the CRA has been obtained.

ReFILE is available in GenuTax Standard when preparing a tax return for any year from 2022 through 2025.

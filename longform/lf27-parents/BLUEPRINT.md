# LF-27 BLUEPRINT — What If You Support Your Parents? (queue #21 + #39, covers #40)

## Hook stage (channel/HOOK-ENGINEERING.md)
**Concept:** money you send to a parent is not, by itself, a tax credit; the Canada caregiver amount depends on the parent's impairment and dependence, not on the money. **Audience:** Canadians aged 30–55 who help their parents with money or care. **Viewer problem:** "I support my mom; can I claim her?" (a common misconception: support = credit).

## Episode
- Story: Maya (Ontario) sends her mother $500/month in 2025. Money alone → no credit. Mother 18+ with an infirmity, dependent on Maya → line 30450 up to $8,601 (2025), reduced by the mother's net income, none at $28,798+; × 14.5% ≈ $1,247. Split with her brother (combined max). Line 30400 eligible dependant (single, lives with you, one per household, reduced by the dependant's income; not both lines for the same person). Provinces differ (ON: no need to live together; AB: must live together). Medical expenses for dependants (line 33199). Missed it → change a past return (ReFILE 2022–2025).
- Mid re-hook ≈60 %: "a bigger amount, and a trap".
- Bridge: #37 benefits your aging parents could be missing.

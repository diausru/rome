## Description
Three people in Ontario sell a home for the same $150,000 profit. One owes $0. One owes $65,669. One owes $29,605. Why?

| Seller | Bucket | Tax | Keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mia (lived in it) | Principal residence exemption | $0 | $150,000 |
| Lena (rented it out 8 years) | Capital gain, half taxable | $29,605 | $120,395 |
| Dev (sold in 10 months) | Flip: business income | $65,669 | $84,331 |

In this video:
• the principal residence exemption, the "only once in a lifetime" myth, and why you must still report the sale (Schedule 3 + Form T2091)
• the flipping rule: owned less than 365 days in a row, sold since 2023 — and the life-event exceptions
• rental homes: half of the capital gain is taxable (the proposed increase was cancelled), plus CCA recapture
• gray zones: moving out and renting, renting part of your home, home + cottage, the plus-one rule
• 4 steps before you sell

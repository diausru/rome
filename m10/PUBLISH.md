The rule (line 32600, Schedule 2): a spouse or common-law partner can claim up to $5,000 of the student's current-year tuition, minus what the student needs to bring their own tax to zero.
• Sam needs $0 → Alex claims $5,000 × 14.5% = $725 off federal tax

The twist: the other $3,000 stays with Sam as a carry-forward and can never be transferred later. Unused age, pension and disability amounts can move to a spouse the same way.

Description:
New to Canada? Some government payments can start soon after you arrive, but only if you apply.

1. Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB): it replaced the GST/HST credit on July 3, 2026. Tax-free, paid every three months (the 5th of January, April, July and October). The amount depends on your family size and income.
2. Canada child benefit (CCB): up to $8,157 a year per child under 6 (July 2026–June 2027), reduced once adjusted family net income passes $38,237. On a work or study permit? You generally need to have lived in Canada for the previous 18 months and hold a valid permit in the 19th month.

How to apply: Form RC151 (no children) or RC66 with Schedule RC66SCH (with children, also covers CGEB), or through your CRA account. You need a social insurance number.

3. Canadian Dental Care Plan: for families with no access to dental insurance and adjusted family net income under $90,000. You must be a resident and have filed a tax return.

The catch: all of them are calculated from your tax return. File every year, even with zero income.

Sources: CRA, Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit; Canada child benefit (how much, who can apply); RC151; RC66; Government of Canada, Canadian Dental Care Plan (canada.ca). The family is an example. Amounts depend on income. General information, not advice.

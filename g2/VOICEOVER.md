## A. Final script
Own an apartment back home? Savings in a foreign bank? CRA may want a form, even if you owe zero tax.
It's form T1135. You file it if your foreign property cost more than one hundred thousand dollars, at any time in the year.
That includes foreign bank accounts, a rental property abroad, even U.S. stocks held at a Canadian broker.
Not included: a vacation home you mainly use yourself, and anything inside your RRSP or TFSA.
It's measured by cost, not today's price. For newcomers, cost is the value on the day you became resident.
And you don't file it for the year you arrive. The rule starts the next year.
File it late, and the penalty is twenty-five dollars a day, up to two thousand five hundred. Even with no tax owing.
Next: leaving Canada? The departure tax. Follow so you don't miss it.

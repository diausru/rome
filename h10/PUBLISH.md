Description:
Your spouse paid no tax this year? Some of their unused credits can move to your return.

• Line 32600: once a spouse's tax is already $0, these can transfer: age amount, pension income amount, disability amount (for self), caregiver amount for an infirm child under 18, and tuition (up to $5,000 of the current year).
• Example: Lee, full-time student, no income, $8,000 tuition. Up to $5,000 moves to the spouse: about $725 off federal tax (14.5% for 2025).

The twist: only this year's tuition can move. Carried-forward amounts stay with Lee.

Use Schedule 2 → line 32600. Not allowed if you were separated 90+ days including Dec 31.

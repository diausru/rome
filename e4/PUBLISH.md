Description:
65 or older on a low income? The Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) is a monthly, tax-free payment on top of OAS, and the government's own estimate says about 240,000 eligible seniors didn't receive it in 2015.

For a single senior (Oct–Dec 2026): up to $1,138.90 a month if your annual income, not counting OAS, is under $23,112. You must receive OAS and live in Canada.
TFSA withdrawals don't count, and the first $5,000 you earn from work doesn't reduce it.
The key: file your taxes every year; that's how eligibility is reviewed. And if you qualify, don't delay OAS: you can't get the GIS while you wait.

Couples have different thresholds. Amounts change every quarter.

Sources: Service Canada, ESDC and CRA (canada.ca). General information, not advice.

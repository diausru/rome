Description:
You filed in March. In June, a slip shows up. File again? No. Here's how fixing a return works.

Case 1: you forgot $1,500 of union dues. Change the return you filed: about $500 comes back ($499 at 33.25%, Manitoba example).
Case 2: a late T4A adds $3,000 of income: you'll owe about $1,000 ($998), plus interest from May 1.
Case 3: a credit you missed years ago: for a refund, you can ask CRA to go back 10 calendar years (a 2026 request reaches back to 2016). It's CRA's call (ITA 152(4.2)).

The twist: you never file a second return. You wait for your notice of assessment, then change the one you filed.

How: Change my return in CRA My Account or ReFILE through your tax software (about 2 weeks), or Form T1-ADJ by mail (about 8 weeks).

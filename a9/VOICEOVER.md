## A. Final script

You filed in March. In June, a slip shows up. File again? No. Here's how fixing a return works. Case one: you forgot fifteen hundred dollars of union dues. Change the return you filed, and about five hundred dollars comes back. Case two: a late T4A adds three thousand dollars of income. You'll owe about a thousand dollars, plus interest from May first. Case three: a credit you missed years ago. For a refund, you can ask CRA to go back ten calendar years. It's CRA's call. The twist: you never file a second return. You wait for your notice of assessment, then change the one you filed. Online, through My Account or ReFILE, takes about two weeks. On paper, form T1-ADJ, about eight. Next: the Manitoba credits people miss on form MB479. Follow so you don't miss it.

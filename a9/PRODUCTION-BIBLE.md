Example (not a CRA rule): Manitoba employee, taxable income about $70,000 (20.5% federal + 12.75% Manitoba bands, 2026). Case 1: $1,500 union dues not claimed → $1,500 × 33.25% = $498.75 (shown $499, spoken "about five hundred"). Case 2: a late T4A with $3,000 of income and no tax withheld → $997.50 (shown $998, spoken "about a thousand") + interest from May 1. Case 3: a credit from years ago → the ten-calendar-year window (a 2026 request reaches back to 2016). The ReFILE year range for 2026 was not confirmed from an official page, so it is not stated. Dependencies: the person's bracket, withholding on the late slip, whether the missed item is a deduction or a credit. Professional-advice territory: deliberate omissions (Voluntary Disclosures Program), reassessments you disagree with (objection), several years at once.

Your first year self-employed in Canada has a tax calendar. Here it is on one page.

• Month one: open a separate tax account (our example sets aside about 27%: $270 from every $1,000).
• Day one: keep every receipt, and a kilometre log if you drive for work. The CRA can ask for records going back six years.
• Every quarter: watch your sales. Over $30,000 in four consecutive quarters → you must register for GST/HST.
• December 31: for most sole proprietors, the business year ends.
• First 60 days of the new year: RRSP contributions can still be deducted for the year that ended.
• April 30: pay your balance. With no instalments yet, the whole bill is due at once.
• June 15: filing deadline for the self-employed. Interest on any unpaid balance already started May 1.
• Year two: if your bill was over $3,000, instalment reminders can start.

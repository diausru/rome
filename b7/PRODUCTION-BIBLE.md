## 2. Facts
| Claim | Source | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Self-employed (and spouse/partner) file by June 15; balance due April 30; tax-shelter exception files April 30 | canada.ca important dates for individuals; CRA newsroom tax tip 2026 (search 2026-10-08) | 2025 tax year (same pattern each year) |
| Daily compound interest on an unpaid balance from the day after it is due (May 1) | CRA wording quoted via search | current |

| Keep records six years from the end of the last tax year they relate to | CRA tax tip "Are you self-employed? Know your tax obligations"; taxtips.ca | current |
| GST/HST: registration required once taxable supplies exceed $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters | b5/ (canada.ca) | current |
| RRSP contributions in the first 60 days of a year can be deducted for the previous year | rrsp-se/ (CRA via secondary summaries) | 2026 |
| Instalments only if net tax owing > $3,000 this year and in one of the two previous years | inst/ (canada.ca) | 2026 |

**Example (not a CRA rule):** "27% → tax acct" = the B4 example (MB, single, $60K net, 2026: ≈$270 per $1,000). "With no instalments yet" is stated as a condition, not a rule for everyone (someone with large past balances may already owe instalments).
**Dependencies:** a non-December fiscal period, a tax-shelter business, Québec (instalment threshold $1,800, provincial return) and HST provinces change details.

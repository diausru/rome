## A. Final script
Why did CRA pick your return? CRA actually publishes the reasons. There are four.
One: your return doesn't match a slip. Employers and banks send CRA copies of your T4s and T5s, and the computer compares them.
Two: the deductions and credits you claimed. Some get a closer look, so keep the receipts.
Three: your history with CRA. Past problems can bring a second look.
Four: pure chance. Some returns are simply picked at random.
And a review is not an audit. CRA reviews about three million returns a year, mostly to confirm your claims can be supported.
If a letter comes, send what they ask for before the deadline. And keep your records for six years.
Next: your first Canadian tax return as a newcomer. Follow so you don't miss it.

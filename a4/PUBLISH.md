Description:
Why did CRA pick your return? CRA publishes the reasons, and there are four.

1. Your return doesn't match a slip: employers and banks send CRA copies of your T4s and T5s, and the matching program compares them with your return.
2. The deductions and credits you claimed: some get a closer look, so keep the receipts.
3. Your compliance history: past problems can bring a second look.
4. Random selection: some returns are picked by chance.

A review is not an audit. CRA reviews about 3 million returns a year, mostly to confirm that income, deductions and credits are reported correctly and can be supported.

If a letter comes: send what they ask for by the date in the letter, and keep your records for six years.

Source: CRA, How tax returns are selected for review; Types of reviews; Keeping records (canada.ca). General information, not advice.

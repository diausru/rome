Break-even (simplified illustration, NOT a CRA/ESDC rule: ignores indexation, taxes, investment returns, survivor and health factors). For $1,000 at 65:
- 60 vs 65: $640 from 60 vs $1,000 from 65 → 640(X−60) = 1000(X−65) → X ≈ 73.9 (age ~74).
- 65 vs 70: $1,420 from 70 vs $1,000 from 65 → 1420(X−70) = 1000(X−65) → X ≈ 81.9 (age ~82).
Dependencies to state: health and life expectancy, other income (OAS clawback, GIS), whether you still work, survivor benefits. Get advice.

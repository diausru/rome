Description:
Two parents, one child, one deposit: every month the Canada Child Benefit lands in Maya's account, never Dan's. It's not a mistake.

The rules (CRA):
• The CRA pays the CCB to only one person per home.
• When both parents live with the child, the female parent is usually considered primarily responsible (a requirement in the Income Tax Act), so she applies.
• If the other parent does most of the caregiving, he can apply instead, with a signed letter from her.
• Same-sex parents: one parent applies for all the children; the couple chooses.

The twist: switching who receives it doesn't change the amount. The CRA says it's the same, no matter which parent receives it. (July 2026–June 2027: up to $8,157 per child under 6, $6,883 per child 6–17, based on adjusted family net income.)

Decide who applies, and keep filing your returns every year.

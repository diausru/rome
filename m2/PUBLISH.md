Income-tested benefits like the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (the old GST credit) and the CCB use family net income: your net income plus your spouse's or common-law partner's.
• Nora, single: $45,000 is under $46,432 → full $679 (July 2026–June 2027).
• Sam: $45,000 + partner's $35,000 = $80,000 family income → $33,568 over the line × 5% = $1,678, more than the $890 couple maximum → $0.

The twist: sometimes it's not income at all. If either spouse didn't file a return, the payments stop.

Check your family net income, and make sure both of you filed.

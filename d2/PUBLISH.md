Description:
Got a raise? Your Canada Child Benefit (CCB) could shrink, but not right away: it runs a year behind.

Payments from July 2026 to June 2027 use your family's 2025 net income. The maximum is $8,157 a year per child under 6 and $6,883 per child aged 6 to 17. Once adjusted family net income passes $38,237, the benefit is reduced (7% of the income above that for one child, 13.5% for two, 19% for three, 23% for four or more, up to $82,847).

Example: one child under 6 and a family net income of $70,000 → about $5,934 a year (≈ $494 a month). A $10,000 raise → about $700 a year less, starting the July after you file. One lever: RRSP contributions lower your net income.

The big rule: you and your spouse or common-law partner must both file a return every year, even with no income, or the payments stop.

Source: CRA, canada.ca (Canada child benefit). The $70,000 family is an example. General information, not advice.

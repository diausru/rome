## 2. Facts
| Claim | Source | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Social-media activity is generally business activity unless a personal endeavour; non-monetary receipts (products, trips, gifts) are income at fair market value | CRA tax tip "Are you a social media influencer?" (canada.ca, via search 2026-10-08) | current |
| GST/HST registration once taxable supplies exceed $30,000 over four calendar quarters | same tip; b5/ | current |
| From calendar 2024, reporting platform operators report reportable sellers (goods, services, rentals) to the CRA by Jan 31 of the next year, copy to sellers | canada.ca Reporting Rules for Digital Platforms; Guidance (Part XX ITA), via search | 2024 → |
| Reasonable expenses to earn business income are deductible | CRA business income (T2125), general | current |

**Example (not a CRA rule):** ads $6,000 + sponsor $2,500 + products kept in exchange for posts $1,500 (FMV) = $10,000 vs cash $8,500.
**Dependencies:** business vs personal endeavour; whether an item was received in exchange for promotion (unsolicited gifts are not addressed by the CRA tip); "many platforms" (only operators meeting Part XX definitions report).
**Not stated (not verified here):** US withholding on AdSense, currency conversion, unsolicited PR packages.

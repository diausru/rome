## A. Final script
Noah is ten. His disability tax credit saves him zero dollars: he pays no tax. So why does his mom want it approved?
Door one: the child disability benefit. Up to three thousand four hundred and eighty dollars a year, paid with the Canada child benefit, less at higher family incomes.
Door two: an RDSP. For lower-income families, a bond of up to a thousand dollars a year, with no contribution. Plus grants of up to thirty-five hundred that match deposits.
Door three, at eighteen: the Canada Disability Benefit. Up to two hundred and four dollars and twenty cents a month, also income-tested.
The twist: every door has the same key. CRA approval of form T twenty-two oh one.
So apply once, and file a tax return every year, even with no income.
Next: the Canada caregiver credit. Follow so you don't miss it.

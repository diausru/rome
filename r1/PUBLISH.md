Description:
Noah is 10. His disability tax credit saves him $0 on his own return: he pays no tax. So why get it approved? Because DTC approval is the key to three other doors.

Door 1: the Child Disability Benefit. Up to $3,480 a year per child (July 2026 – June 2027), paid with the Canada child benefit; reduced when family net income is over $82,847.
Door 2: an RDSP. For lower-income families, the Canada Disability Savings Bond pays up to $1,000 a year with no contribution (lifetime $20,000). Grants match deposits up to $3,500 a year (lifetime $70,000).
Door 3, at 18: the Canada Disability Benefit. Up to $204.20 a month (July 2026 – June 2027), ages 18–64, income-tested.

The key for all three: CRA approval of form T2201. Then file a tax return every year, even with no income.

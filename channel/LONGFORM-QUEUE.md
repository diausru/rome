## Tax software review format (topics #2, #29–#36; user, 2026-10-07)
- NOT a generic sponsored-looking ad: an independent comparison based on current research and the viewer's situation, not on sponsorship.
- Criteria: current 2026 tax-season availability; CRA certification / NETFILE eligibility; price; free vs paid features; CRA Auto-fill my return; ease of use; self-employment support; investment and capital-gains support; rental income; RRSP optimization; provincial returns; expert assistance; audit support; mobile vs desktop; privacy / data considerations; best use case for different Canadians.
- Candidates: Wealthsimple Tax, TurboTax Canada, H&R Block Canada, UFile, StudioTax, CloudTax, GenuTax, other CRA-certified products when relevant (the CRA's certified-software list is the primary source for certification).
- If a company sponsors an episode: clear disclosure and full editorial independence.
- Goal: genuinely useful to Canadians, and through that, show TAX SECRETS CANADA as a credible platform for tax software, fintech, accounting, banking, investing and personal-finance advertisers.

# PUBLISHING & GROWTH ENGINE — MANDATORY (user rule, 2026-10-05)

Mandatory post-production and distribution system for EVERY Tax Secrets Canada video. Rendering the MP4 is NOT the end of the task. After the video passes visual, technical, factual and voiceover QC, switch into PUBLISHING & GROWTH MODE and prepare the complete publication package for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, ready to copy and paste with minimal editing.

Pipeline: RESEARCH → CREATE → RENDER → QC → OPTIMIZE → PUBLISH

1. **Research the current content landscape** for the exact topic: Canadian tax and finance discussions, YouTube search intent, TikTok search and discovery patterns, Instagram/Reels search behaviour, current Canadian tax news, current audience questions, high-performing content, seasonal interest, emerging issues and the audience's own terminology. Tax facts come from CRA, the Government of Canada, provinces and StatCan. Audience research comes from current public platform and search signals and reputable sources. Never copy another creator's wording, visuals, title or structure: study the market, extract what works, make an ORIGINAL Tax Secrets Canada version.
2. **Search intent:** primary search intent; primary keyword; secondary keywords; search questions; audience; pain or curiosity. No keyword stuffing; keywords appear naturally in the title, description, speech and on-screen text where appropriate.
3. **Retention analysis:** hook strength (1–2 s), open loop, information density, visual change rate, escalation, reveal, payoff, CTA. If weak, FIX the script, timing, visuals or structure and re-render. Don't just report it.
4. **Engagement engine:** primary comment question; pinned comment; save trigger; share trigger; follow reason. No empty bait ("Comment YES", "Like if you agree", "Follow for more") unless genuinely justified. Engagement comes from the information.
5. **YouTube Shorts package:** primary title + alternative titles A and B (curiosity, clarity, search relevance, Canadian context, information gap, no misleading clickbait); full description (first 1–2 lines matter most); relevant hashtags (not a giant block); keywords/tags; exact pinned comment; CTA.
6. **TikTok package:** a short, searchable, curiosity caption whose first line is a hook; natural-language search keywords; current relevant hashtags (not a copy of YouTube's); a discussion pinned comment; a CTA that doesn't sound like an ad.
7. **Instagram Reels package:** an Instagram-native caption with a curiosity first line and natural keywords; Instagram hashtags; a pinned comment; ONE fitting CTA (comment / save / share / follow; don't force all four).
8. **Platform optimization:** the same facts, different packaging. YouTube = SEARCH + RETENTION + INFORMATION; TikTok = DISCOVERY + CURIOSITY + DISCUSSION; Instagram = SAVES + SHARES + DISCOVERY.
9. **Title/hook QC:** understandable? curious enough? an information gap? not misleading? natural? search language? immediately relevant to a Canadian? Reject weak titles.
10. **Fact-check the package:** exactly the same claims as the video (amounts, percentages, rates, dates, thresholds, rankings, comparisons, eligibility, statistics, programs). No new unverified claims in captions. If a number changes, update VIDEO + VOICEOVER + ON-SCREEN TEXT + DESCRIPTION + CAPTIONS together.
11. **Source package:** for each material claim, give SOURCE · DATE / TAX YEAR · WHAT IT SUPPORTS. Primary sources first; no random blogs when an official source exists.
12. **Content opportunity analysis:** NEXT 3 HIGH-POTENTIAL TOPICS, each with topic, why it matters, primary search intent, hook idea, curiosity angle and potential payoff, building a connected ecosystem.
13. **Series strategy** when justified (e.g. Canadian Tax Myths · CRA Red Flags · Tax Mistakes Canadians Make · Province vs Province · Things Your Accountant Wants You To Know · Canadian Tax Changes · Can You Deduct This? · Real Tax Math). Don't force it.

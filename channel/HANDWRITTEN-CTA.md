The CTA must feel like part of the handwritten explanation — NOT like an advertisement or a generic social-media outro.

The hand should physically write the final CTA on the same paper/environment used throughout the video.

The visual sequence should be:

→
SHORT PAUSE
→

→
CURIOUS QUESTION OR CHALLENGE
→
UNDERLINE / CIRCLE / ARROW
→
1–1.5 SECOND HOLD
→
END

Preferred CTA strategy:

Do NOT rely on:

"Follow for more."
"Like and subscribe."
"Thanks for watching."

Instead create a curiosity loop that gives the viewer a reason to interact.

"Ask us in the comments."

"Your question could be our next video."

The exact wording must be adapted to the topic.

VISUAL RULES:

1. The handwritten CTA must use the SAME hand, pen, paper, lighting, camera angle and physical environment.
2. The hand must actually write the words.
3. Do NOT fade the CTA onto the screen as a digital overlay.
4. Do NOT use a generic end card.
5. Do NOT suddenly change the environment.
6. Do NOT introduce a new character.
7. The handwriting must remain physically believable:
    * natural stroke order
    * realistic pen pressure
    * small imperfections
    * natural writing speed
    * realistic pauses
    * authentic underlining/circling
8. The final CTA should visually interact with the previous calculation.

Examples:

Circle the final number.
Draw an arrow from the final result to the question.
Underline the final question.
Add a small handwritten question mark.

RETENTION RULE:

The ending should create an OPEN LOOP whenever possible.

The viewer should finish the Short thinking:

"I want to know that."

or:

"I should ask this."

or:

"I want to see the next breakdown."

The CTA should therefore connect the current topic to the next logical tax question.

Weak:

Instead of:

or:

"Follow — your question could be next."

## 2. Cadence and timing [Plan]
- **3 Shorts a week (Tue / Thu / Sat)** for the first 16 weeks covers all 48 topics by late January, just before tax season. Keep a 2-Short buffer rendered ahead.
- Time: start at 12:00 Central (Winnipeg). After 3 weeks, switch to the peak shown in Studio → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube".
- Do not delete or re-upload Shorts that underperform. Read "viewed vs. swiped away" and average % viewed after 48 h, and fix the hook on the next one.

## 3. Every upload [Plan, built on the facts above]
- **Title (≤ 60 characters, the keyword first; no hashtags in the title):** a question or a number, e.g. "$100K salary: what you keep in each province (2026)".
- **Description:** line 1 restates the answer in one sentence. Then the assumptions ("2026 · single · employment income…") and sources (the same as the video footer). Then "General info, not advice." Then 3–5 hashtags: `#CanadaTax #CRA #PersonalFinanceCanada` plus 1–2 topic tags (e.g. `#RRSP`).
- **Related video:** the next Short in the same playlist (later: the matching long-form video).
- **Pinned comment:** one question that invites a reply ("Which province are you in?"). Topic 0.2 (viewer questions) feeds on these replies.
- **Playlists:** one per TOPICS section (A Employees, B Self-employed, C Business, D Families, E Retirees, F Investors, G Newcomers, H Manitoba).
- **Series tag in the frame:** the header already shows TAX SECRETS CANADA, the flag and the tax year. Every video carries its sources footer.
- **Cross-post** the same file to Instagram Reels (@canadataxwizard) the same day; the 4:5 carousel is optional for evergreen topics.

**1. Refund or Owe? Why Two People With the Same Salary Get Opposite Results**
- Hook: same salary, one gets money back, one owes.
- Promise: a refund is not a bonus; it is over-withholding, and you'll see where the gap comes from.
- Investigation: (a) one employer, standard TD1 → close to zero; (b) RRSP contribution → refund; (c) side income or two employers → owing. Documents: T4 box 22, NOA line 48400/48500.
- Reveal: the person who "won" just lent CRA money interest-free.
- Action: compare T4 box 22 with the tax on your return; adjust TD1 or request reduced withholding (T1213) `[VERIFY form use]`.

**2. Same Income, No Benefits? Why Your Friend Gets Paid and You Don't** (absorbs #19)
- Hook: same salary, the friend gets CCB/GST credit, you get nothing.
- Promise: which number CRA actually looks at (family net income, not salary) and why filing matters.
- Investigation: (a) single vs couple; (b) RRSP lowering net income; (c) not filed → nothing. Benefits finder as the closing tool.
- Reveal: the difference is often the spouse's income or an unfiled return, not the salary.
- Action: check the benefit's income test and that both spouses filed.

**3. CCB Goes to Mom, Not Dad? The CRA Caregiver Rule**
- Hook: the money lands in her account, never his.
- Promise: who CRA presumes is the primary caregiver and when that changes.
- Investigation: (a) two-parent home (presumption) `[VERIFY wording]`; (b) the father is the main caregiver (how to apply); (c) same-sex parents.
- Reveal: the amount depends on family income, not on who receives it.
- Action: check who is registered (My Account) and whether that matches reality.

**4 → R1. Disability Tax Credit: Why Approval Can Mean Years of Refunds** `[VERIFY]`
- Hook: one approved form, several years of refunds.
- Promise: who qualifies, the doctor's part, and how CRA adjusts past years.
- Investigation: adult approved for prior years; child (with CCB disability benefit); transfer to a family member.
- Reveal: the approval is about the effect on daily life, not the diagnosis.
- Action: check eligibility questions and whether past years can be adjusted.

**5. Two Jobs, One Surprise Bill: Why You Owe in April**

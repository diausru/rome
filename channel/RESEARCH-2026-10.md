## 3. What the data shows

1. **The audience is retirees and pre-retirees.** CPP, OAS, RRSP→RRIF, the TFSA in retirement, and "working past 65" draw the most views. Tax content wins when it is framed as **a money decision**, not as tax.
2. **Winning title shapes:**
   - a **mistake**: "Most Canadians make this TFSA mistake";
   - **what happens if**: "What happens to your RRSP at 71";
   - a **benchmark**: "average balances by age";
   - **this year's changes**: "Tax changes in Canada for 2026".
3. **Length:** the winners run 8–20 minutes.
4. **Most of the fast growers are AI-voiced faceless channels** using the same template: stock footage or slides, a 🎉/⚠️ emoji, and "quietly / CRA doesn't want you to know / invisible to CRA" phrasing. That is exactly the conspiratorial framing your own files ban (06 v2, 07). **It is also the gap:** nobody in this wave is a real accountant showing real calculations.
5. Small channels can break out: Sim Gakhar (4.6K subs) had a video at 48× his channel average with a simple "what happens at 71" question.

## 4. Implications for Tax Secrets Canada
- Lead with the retirement and money-decision topics already in TOPICS.md: E1–E4, D6, A2, A3.
- Make the difference visible: **a working accountant does the math on screen**. Do not out-shout the AI channels; show them the calculation.
- Keep the files' rule: no "secret / loophole / they don't want you to know" hooks.

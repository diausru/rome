Description:
Freelancing in Canada and getting close to $30,000? The GST/HST threshold isn't counted per calendar year. It's any four consecutive calendar quarters.

Example: $5,000 + $8,000 + $9,000 + $9,500 over four quarters = $31,500, over the line. You stop being a small supplier at the end of the month after that quarter, then you have 29 days to register. Cross $30,000 in a single quarter and you charge GST/HST on the very sale that put you over.

After that, a $1,000 invoice in Manitoba carries $50 of GST. That $50 was never yours. The upside: GST/HST you pay on business costs comes back as input tax credits. HST provinces charge 13–15%, and provincial sales taxes have their own rules.

Source: CRA, "When to register for and start charging the GST/HST" (canada.ca). Example only; general information, not tax advice.

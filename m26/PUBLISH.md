Description:
Same child, two homes, two deposits. After a separation, both parents can get the Canada Child Benefit, but not always the same amount.

The rule (CRA): if the child lives with you 40% to 60% of the time, it's shared custody. Each parent gets 50% of the payment they would get if the child lived with them full-time, and each half is calculated on that parent's own adjusted family net income. So the lower-income parent's half can be bigger.

Example: a 5-year-old, week on / week off. July 2026–June 2027 maximum for a child under 6: $8,157 a year, so each half is at most $4,078.50.

The twist: over 60% of the time, you have full custody and you're the only one eligible. Under 40%, you can't apply.

Count the real nights before either of you applies.

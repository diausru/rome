### Dependencies, exceptions, notes
- The R4–R5 conditions are from the 2025 CRA package (the most recent published); the 2026 forms will be published for the 2027 filing season. Stated as current rules; the user should re-check the 2026 MB479 when it's released.
- Seniors' top-up eligibility details (age, income test) weren't confirmed in this pass → "for eligible seniors", with no criteria stated.
- Spouses, personal care homes, separate residences for medical reasons: special rules (R5 source), not covered.

### Claims deliberately NOT made
- The old "20% of rent" formula (an Education Property Tax Credit-era formula; not confirmed for the new credit).
- Any income test for the renters credit itself.

Description:
Rent in Manitoba? There's a credit on your tax return you don't want to miss, and it went up.

The Renters Affordability Tax Credit: up to $625 for the 2026 tax year (it was up to $575 for 2025). You need to live in Manitoba on December 31, be at least 16, and pay rent on your main home in Manitoba. Moved during the year? You claim the months you rented. Eligible seniors can also get a top-up of up to $357.

You claim it on Form MB479 (Manitoba Credits) with your return, so keep your rent records.

Description

Self-employed in Canada? Nobody withholds tax for you, so here's the real math on a $1,000 invoice. Example (2026, Manitoba, single, $60,000 net self-employment income, no other income or deductions): CPP (both halves, 11.9%): $6,724 Federal tax: $5,155 Manitoba tax: $4,227 Total: about $16,106, roughly 27% of net income. But that's the average. In this example, each extra dollar costs about 36¢. A simple habit: move about $270 of every $1,000 into a separate tax account the day you're paid. And if your net tax owing is over $3,000 this year and in one of the last two, you may have to pay by instalments. Different province or income = different numbers. Sources: CRA (canada.ca, line 22200; required tax instalments), CRA T4127 2026. General information, not tax advice. #SelfEmployed #CanadaTax #CRA

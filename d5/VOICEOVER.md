## A. Final script
Paying for daycare? Part of it can come off your taxable income.
But usually, only one parent can claim it.
Daycare centres, nursery schools, caregivers and day camps can all count.
The limit: eight thousand a year per child under seven, five thousand from seven to sixteen.
Generally, the parent with the lower net income claims it.
Say one parent earns ninety thousand, the other thirty, and daycare cost nine thousand. The lower earner claims eight thousand.
There's also a cap: two-thirds of that parent's earned income.
The higher earner claims only in specific cases, like when the other parent was in school or in hospital.
Keep every receipt. For a babysitter, get their social insurance number on it.
And because it lowers net income, it can help your child benefit too.
Next: why a small corporation pays just nine percent. Follow so you don't miss it.

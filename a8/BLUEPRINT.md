# A8 — Retention Blueprint (written before any visuals)

**Opening question:** can't pay your tax bill, so is it safer not to file?
**Promised result:** what late filing actually costs, compared with filing on time and not paying, and the two dates that matter for 2026 returns.

**First 30 s (VO → visual):**
- 0–2 s "Can't pay your tax bill, so you don't file?" → frame 0: "CAN'T PAY? / DON'T FILE?" in large type; tags LATE-FILING PENALTY and APRIL 30 float in.
- 2–5 s "That's the move that costs extra." → a red COSTS EXTRA stamp lands on the question.
- 5–7 s "Three returns. Same deadline." → three paper returns drop in (OWES, OWES, REFUND); SAME DEADLINE · APRIL 30.
- 8–20 s Return 1: $4,000 owing, 4 months late → the month strip lights MAY–AUG; chips 5% + 1% × 4 = 9%; $360 counts up; the scoreboard logs $360.
- 20–30 s Return 2: same $4,000, filed on time, not paid → ✓ FILED BY APRIL 30; $0 penalty; an interest dash line starts running from May 1.

**Open questions carried:** after return 1 ("is late always worse?"), return 2 shows the same debt with no penalty; return 3 asks "what if I'm owed money?".
**Reveal (39–54 s):** split screen NOT FILING vs NOT PAYING; the gauge climbs to 9% and then to the 17% maximum ($680 on the example); repeat penalty 10% + 2%/month (× 2) with its two conditions.
**Answer + next step (54–65 s):** two tickets: APR 30, 2027 (file, even if you can't pay) and JUN 15, 2027 for the self-employed and their spouse (but pay by APR 30); on-screen: ask CRA about a payment arrangement. Bridge to A2 (why your refund got smaller).
**Verified calculations and sources:** `a8/data.json` (ITA 150(1)(d), 162(1)–(2), 122.5(3), 161(1), 248(1); canada.ca late-filing penalty page via search results). Each return lands its result within ~10 s; nothing is delayed.

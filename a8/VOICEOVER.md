## A. Final script

Can't pay your tax bill, so you don't file? That's the move that costs extra. Three returns. Same deadline. Return one owes four thousand dollars and files four months late. Five percent, plus one percent per full month: three hundred and sixty dollars, before interest. Return two owes the same, files on time, can't pay. Late-filing penalty: zero. Interest still runs from May first. Return three is due a refund and files late. No penalty. But GST credit payments can stop until it's filed. The twist: the penalty is for not filing, not for not paying. After a year, it hits seventeen percent. Repeat it within three years, after a CRA demand, and the rates double. File by April thirtieth, even if you can't pay. Self-employed? File by June fifteenth, but still pay by April thirtieth. Next: why your refund got smaller. Follow so you don't miss it.

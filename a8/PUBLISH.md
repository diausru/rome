Description:
Can't pay your tax bill, so you don't file? That's the move that costs extra. Three returns, same deadline:

• Return 1 owes $4,000 and files 4 months late: 5% + 1% per full month = 9% = $360 late-filing penalty, plus interest.
• Return 2 owes the same $4,000, files on time, can't pay: $0 late-filing penalty. Interest still runs from May 1.
• Return 3 is due a refund and files late: $0 penalty (nothing was owed), but GST/HST credit payments can stop until the return is filed.

The twist: the penalty is for not filing, not for not paying. After 12 months it reaches 17% of the unpaid tax. Late again within three years, after a CRA demand to file, and the rates double (10% + 2% a month, up to 20 months).

For 2026 returns: file by April 30, 2027. Self-employed (and your spouse or common-law partner): file by June 15, 2027, but pay by April 30. Can't pay in full? Ask CRA about a payment arrangement.

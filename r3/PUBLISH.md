Description:
Same $400 to charity: $58 back one way, $87 the other.

The rule (federal, 2025): the donation credit is 14.5% on the first $200 of gifts and 29% on every dollar above $200. You or your spouse or common-law partner can claim the couple's eligible gifts.
• Separately: Alex $200 → $29, Sam $200 → $29 = $58
• One return: $400 → $29 + $58 = $87 (+$29)

The twist: unused donations can be carried forward up to 5 years, so small gifts can be claimed together once they pass $200.

Pool your receipts before you file (Schedule 9, line 34900).

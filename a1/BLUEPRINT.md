# A1 — Retention Blueprint (written before any visuals)

**Opening question:** One family, three receipts in a drawer: how much were they about to leave unclaimed?
**Promised result:** you'll know which credits pay you back even when you owe no tax, and where to check yours.

**First 30 s (VO → visual):**
- 0–5 s "One family. Three receipts. Four thousand, five hundred and fifty dollars they almost left with the CRA." → three manila case folders slam onto the frame, a gold counter races to $4,550 (topic + number on frame 0).
- 5–8 s "Here's which credits pay you back, even when you owe no tax." → counter shrinks into a top strip; "REFUNDABLE?" tag.
- 8–18 s Case 1, teacher, $1,000 of supplies × 25% → $250 refundable (folder opens, receipt line prints, stamp REFUNDABLE).
- 18–30 s Case 2, she's 40, $3,000 course; training credit room +$250 a year since 2020 → claims half the fee, $1,500, refundable (room bar fills year by year).

**Developments / open questions:** after case 1 → "is that the biggest one?"; after case 2 → "and the one worth the most?"; case 3 (mom, 70, $20,000 accessibility renovation × 14% = $2,800) → stacked blocks reach $4,550; reveal answers why it's not "free money" for everyone.

**Reveal:** two of the three are refundable (paid even at $0 tax); the biggest one is non-refundable (only reduces tax owed). The training room sits unused on the notice of assessment.

**Final answer + next step:** $4,550 across three credits, with conditions. Action: check the training credit limit on your notice of assessment / My Account; keep receipts. Bridge: next, "CRA says you owe: what to check first" (A5, list-2 #6).

**Verified calculations and sources:** `a1/data.json` (ITA 122.9, 122.91, 118.5(1.2), 118.041, 117(2), 248(1); canada.ca line 45350 for the NOA). No answer is delayed artificially: each case lands its number within ~8 s of its start.

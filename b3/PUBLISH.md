Description:
Self-employed and working from home? Your rent isn't the write-off. The business share of your home is, if the space qualifies.

The test: the workspace is your main place of business, or it's used only for business and to meet clients regularly. Example: 120 sq ft office in a 1,000 sq ft apartment = 12%. Rent, utilities and insurance of $20,200 × 12% = $2,424 deduction, about $862 of tax and CPP saved in our 2026 Manitoba example ($60K net self-employment income).

Three limits: shared space is cut again by business hours; the claim can't create a business loss (the extra carries forward); and owners should get advice before claiming depreciation (CCA) on the home.

This covers the self-employed only; employees have different rules. Source: CRA, business-use-of-home expenses (canada.ca). Example only; general information, not tax advice.

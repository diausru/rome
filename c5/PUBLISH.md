Description:
Four money moves expire on December 31 in Canada, and one deadline almost everyone gets wrong.

1. Donations: gifts made by December 31 count on this year's return. Keep the official receipt.
2. TFSA: need money early next year? Withdraw in December. The room comes back January 1, not a whole year later (withdraw in January and you wait until the next January 1).
3. FHSA: first-time home buyer? Open an FHSA before the year ends, even empty: that starts $8,000 of participation room, and unused room carries forward (up to $8,000).
4. RESP: contribute $2,500 for a child by December 31 and the basic Canada Education Savings Grant adds $500 for the year (missed years carry forward, max $1,000 of grant a year).

The myth: the RRSP deadline isn't December 31. Contributions in the first 60 days of the new year still count for this year, unless you turn 71 this year (then it's December 31).

Sources: CRA (gifts and income tax, TFSA withdrawals, FHSA participation room, RRSP important dates) and ESDC (CESG), canada.ca. General information, not advice.

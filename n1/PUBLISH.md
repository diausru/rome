2027 tax changes: federal amounts are indexed, Manitoba's are not.

• Federal basic personal amount (max): $16,452 (2026) → $16,879 (2027). +$427 × 14% ≈ $60 less federal tax.
• First federal bracket ends at $60,044 in 2027 (was $58,523).
• Proposed: the base CPP rate falls from 4.95% to 4.75% (employee). 0.20% × ($76,700 − $3,500) = up to $146.40 a year. Only if enacted as proposed.
• Manitoba: no indexing for 2027. The provincial basic personal amount stays $15,780.

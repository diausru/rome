## A. Final script
New to Canada? Your first tax return works differently. Here's what changes.
You become a resident when you set up real ties here: a home, a spouse, a life. Usually the day you arrive.
From that day, you report your world income, from every country. Before it, Canada generally doesn't tax your foreign income.
But you still list what you earned abroad, up to two years back, so CRA can work out your benefits.
Your credits shrink to match. Arrive in September, and the basic personal amount is cut to about a third.
Don't wait for the return. Apply for benefits right away, with form RC151, or RC66 if you have kids.
Then file by April thirtieth, every year, even with no income, or the payments stop.
Next: the benefits newcomers can claim. Follow so you don't miss it.

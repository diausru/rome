Description:
Turning 71 this year? Your RRSP has to be closed by December 31: cash it out, buy an annuity, or transfer it to a RRIF.

Cash out: the whole amount is income that year, and on a lump sum over $15,000 the issuer withholds 30% (outside Québec). On $300,000 that's $90,000 withheld up front, and it isn't even the final tax. Annuity or RRIF: no tax is withheld on the direct transfer; you're taxed as the payments come in. RRIF withdrawals start the year after; at 72 the minimum is 5.40% of the value (on $300,000: $16,200), with no tax withheld on the minimum, but it's still income.

The real danger is doing nothing: if the plan no longer qualifies as an RRSP, CRA treats you as having received the full value as income.

Choosing or mixing options depends on your situation. Talk to your issuer and a tax professional.
